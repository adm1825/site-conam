Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy is important to us. Conambr Tech Services LLC respects your privacy regarding any information we may collect through our portfolio of applications (including, but not limited to, Helper and HelperClean).

Target Audience: Our applications are designed strictly for Business-to-Business (B2B) use, specifically for employers, contractors, and facility managers looking to hire workforce. They are not intended for consumer use.

Location Use: For the core functioning of connecting contractors and helpers in the same region, we request access to device location data. This is used exclusively to display available professionals within the defined distance radius.

Profile Data: User information, work history, and reviews are visible to other platform users to establish professional trust.

SMS Communications: If you opt in during app registration, we may send you SMS notifications related to work proposal updates and account alerts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Mobile phone numbers collected for SMS purposes are not shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

WhatsApp Groups Feature (optional): If you choose to connect your WhatsApp number in the app, we read the messages of the WhatsApp groups that you select in order to find job offers and people available to work, and we post your offers to the groups that you select. We collect and process: the names and sizes of your groups, the text of messages in the selected groups that appear to be job offers or availability notices, the display name and phone number of the person who posted each message (who may not be a user of our apps), the time of the post, and the status of your connection. We do not store messages from groups that you have not selected. Messages are kept for 24 hours and then deleted; if you disconnect WhatsApp in the app, your connection and group data are removed. To classify the messages, their text is sent to third-party artificial intelligence services (Google), and our messaging and hosting infrastructure providers process the data on our behalf. We do not sell this information. This feature is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta, and connecting a number this way is not authorized by WhatsApp, so the connected number may be restricted or banned. You can turn this feature off at any time.

To request the deletion of your account or data from any of our apps, contact us via the email provided in the support section.
